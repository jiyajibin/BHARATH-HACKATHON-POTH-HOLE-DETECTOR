RoadGuard 🚧
AI-Powered Pothole Detection and Road Safety System
RoadGuard is a smart road-safety application designed to identify potholes, record their locations, and help users stay informed about damaged roads.

Problem Statement
Potholes and damaged roads can create safety risks for drivers and other road users. Manual reporting can be slow and may not provide accurate location information.

Our Solution
RoadGuard aims to use artificial intelligence, GPS technology, and community reporting to identify and track road hazards and budget friendly tool that can be used by anyone through this vehicle

Key Features
AI-based pothole detection
GPS-based location tracking
Road hazard mapping
Community pothole reporting
Voice-based warnings
Road safety dashboard
Road condition analytics
